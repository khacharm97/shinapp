About Us

ShinApp: The new digital platform for the construction industry

Construction and renovation are among the most important and responsible processes in life. Whether you're renovating an apartment, building a home, or designing a commercial space, successful outcomes always depend on choosing the right specialist.

However, today this process is often complicated: people spend hours or even days finding reliable specialists, comparing offers, understanding pricing, and searching for various services. On the other hand, experienced professionals, crews, and small companies don't always have the opportunity to showcase themselves to new clients and grow their business.

The ShinApp platform was created to solve exactly this problem.

We want to build a single digital environment where all participants in the construction industry can easily find each other, collaborate, and execute successful projects.

Our Goal

ShinApp's goal is to digitize the construction sector and make collaboration as simple, fast, and transparent as possible.

We want anyone to be able to find the specialist they need in just a few minutes, view their experience, past work, and ratings, and get in touch immediately.

At the same time, we aim to create an environment where specialists can present their skills, build a professional reputation, and get new orders—without intermediaries.

What does ShinApp offer?

ShinApp is not just a directory of specialists. It's a multifunctional platform that brings the entire construction ecosystem together in one place. On the platform, you can:

Find experienced specialists,

Select complete construction crews,

Discover architects and interior designers,

Rent construction equipment,

Post or find job advertisements,

Explore the portfolios of professionals,

Compare ratings and reviews,

Filter by location, experience, and availability.

All this on one platform—fast and convenient.

Our Values

Trust

We believe that trust is the foundation of successful collaboration. That's why every specialist can showcase their portfolio, work experience, services, and customer ratings.

Transparency

Our aim is for clients to have as much information as possible to make the right decision before initiating contact.

Quality

We encourage high professional quality and create an environment where the best specialists can receive the recognition they deserve for their work.

Accessibility

We want everyone, regardless of their location or the size of their project, to easily find the services they need.

Who is ShinApp for?

ShinApp is designed for anyone connected in any way to the construction and renovation industry.

For Clients

If you want to renovate an apartment, build a house, or carry out any construction project, ShinApp lets you find trusted specialists and teams without long searches.

For Specialists

If you work as an electrician, plumber, painter, tiler, architect, designer, or other specialist, ShinApp gives you the opportunity to create a professional page, present your work, and get new orders.

For Crews

Teams can have a single shared profile, present all their members, and take on larger projects.

For Construction Companies

Companies can present their services, post job listings, and expand their teams with new professionals.

For Equipment Owners

Owners of construction machinery and equipment can showcase their inventory, set rental conditions, and easily find clients.

Our Vision

We believe that the construction industry must also embrace modern digital solutions.

Our vision is to build Armenia's largest and most reliable construction platform, uniting all industry participants into one community.

In the future, ShinApp plans to introduce new features such as online bookings, smart search, automated estimates, digital contracts, a payment system, and many more industry-specific services.

Our Mission

We believe that right collaborations begin with the right connections.

ShinApp's mission is to create a platform where every client finds a reliable specialist, and every specialist finds new opportunities.

We are building not just a technological platform, but a community where professional experience, honest work, and long-term partnerships are valued.
